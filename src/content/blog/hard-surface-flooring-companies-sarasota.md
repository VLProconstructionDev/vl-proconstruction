## How we ranked these companies

1. **Who does the work** — in-house crews, owner-supervised, or day-labor subs? The single
   biggest predictor of quality.
2. **Subfloor prep as a line item** — if moisture testing, grinding, and self-levelling
   aren't in the written estimate, they become change orders or get skipped.
3. **Written workmanship warranty** — the installer's own warranty, not the
   manufacturer's, is the one that matters.
4. **Licensed and insured** — ask for the certificate, not the claim.
5. **Itemized pricing** — a lump-sum number hides where the money goes.

## Five questions to ask before you sign

1. **Will you moisture-test my slab, and will the reading be in writing?** The only
   acceptable answer is yes, with a number.
2. **Who is physically installing this — your employees or subcontractors?** And will it be
   the same crew the whole job?
3. **Is subfloor prep in this estimate or extra?** Grinding and self-levelling a Florida
   slab is real money. Find out now, not on day two.
4. **What's your written workmanship warranty, in years?** Get the document. Same for the
   license and certificate of insurance.
5. **Show me an itemized estimate.** Demo and haul-away, prep, membrane, material, labor,
   trim, transitions — each on its own line.

## FAQs

**Is engineered hardwood or solid hardwood better in Sarasota?**
Engineered, in almost every case. Real wood on a dimensionally stable core handles the
humidity cycle far better over a slab, and it can be glued directly down. Solid hardwood
over slab-on-grade needs a plywood sleeper system, which adds cost and height.

**Can I install new flooring over existing tile?**
Sometimes — it comes down to finished height, substrate integrity, and moisture. If
someone answers this over the phone without seeing it, be skeptical.

**Do I need a permit for flooring in Sarasota County?**
Straight floor covering replacement generally doesn't. It changes if you're altering
structure, moving plumbing, or working in a condo — most associations set their own
sound-transmission (IIC/STC) minimums for hard surface floors above the ground floor.
Check your association documents before you buy material.

**How long does a flooring install take?**
For a typical 1,200–1,800 sq ft Sarasota home: 3–5 days for LVP, 4–7 for engineered
hardwood, longer if the slab needs levelling or the material is still acclimating.
