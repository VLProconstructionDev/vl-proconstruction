faqs:
  - q: How long does a bathroom remodel take?
    a: A standard hall bath runs 3–5 weeks from demo to final walkthrough. A large primary suite with a wet room and custom glass runs 6–8 weeks. Tile and vanity lead times are the usual variable, so we confirm every material before demo starts.
  - q: What does a bathroom remodel cost in Bradenton or Sarasota?
    a: Most remodels in this market land between $18,000 and $45,000 depending on the size of the room, whether plumbing moves, and the tile you choose. We price our scope up front – no allowances that balloon later – and any plumbing, electrical, or permit work is priced by the general contractor we bring in.
  - q: Do I need a permit?
    a: Yes, for anything that moves plumbing or electrical. We are not a general contractor and don't pull permits, so for those jobs the licensed general contractor we work with files with Manatee or Sarasota County and handles the inspections; a cosmetic swap that keeps every fixture in place usually does not need one.
  - q: Can I use my bathroom during the remodel?
    a: Not the one we are working in. Most homes have a second bath, and we sequence the work so you are never without one. If yours is the only bathroom, tell us early and we will plan the schedule around it.
  - q: Do you move plumbing to change the layout?
    a: Not ourselves – we don't do plumbing or electrical work. Relocating a toilet, shifting a vanity to a double, or converting a tub alcove into a walk-in shower all mean new rough-in, so we bring in the general contractor we work with or recommend a licensed plumber, and do the tile, waterproofing, and glass around it.
